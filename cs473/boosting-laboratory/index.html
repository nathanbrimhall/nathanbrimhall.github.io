Boosting laboratory

Three ways to build an ensemble one small correction at a time.

All observations are fabricated, reproducible simulations. Models fit only the filled training points; outlined validation points measure performance and never enter fitting. One input feature makes the entire learned function visible.

Settings apply to all three trajectories. Change a setting to restart them.

Compare the steps

These stages correspond by purpose, not by number: some calculations have no direct counterpart. A round means one learner addition; the same round number does not imply equal optimization progress.

AdaBoost’s weight-multiplier step has no direct least-squares counterpart. LogitBoost’s curvature and Newton working response have no direct AdaBoost counterpart.

AdaBoost’s σ(2F) is an exponential-loss-implied mapping, not a calibration guarantee. Regression squared loss and classification log loss use different units and scales. Random forests fit independent trees; boosting adds sequential corrections.

Move a split

Temperature is the horizontal axis and vibration is vertical. The dashed line sends observations on its left to the left child.

ΔI = I(parent) − (nL/n) I(left) − (nR/n) I(right). Here I is the chosen impurity, n is the parent count, and nL and nR are child counts. Weighting gives each observation equal influence. In the preset, both children still predict defective, so misclassification impurity does not change; Gini and entropy also reward the increased class concentration.

One split at a time

    Each position selects a weakest-link subtree; α is its effective penalty threshold.

    Filled symbols: training; outlined symbols: validation. ◆ is the movable test product.

    Rα(T) = R(T) + αL(T): α is the penalty per leaf, L(T) is the number of leaves, and R(T) is the sum of leaf Gini impurities weighted by each leaf’s fraction of all training observations. Classification error counts wrong predictions; it is different from Gini impurity.

    For a subtree Tt rooted at node t, αeff(t) = [R(t) − R(Tt)] / [L(Tt) − 1]. R(t) treats that subtree as one leaf; R(Tt) is its current weighted impurity using the same full-training-set denominator; L(Tt) is its leaf count. The smallest effective α is pruned first. Validation or cross-validation can choose a pruning strength; pruning does not always improve validation error.

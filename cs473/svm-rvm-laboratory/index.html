    Equations and fitting details

    Both scores use k(xᵢ,x) = exp(−γ‖xᵢ−x‖²). The SVM fits the soft-margin objective with sequential minimal optimization. Its signed weight is αᵢyᵢ, with 0 ≤ αᵢ ≤ C. Its hinge loss is max(0, 1 − yᵢf(xᵢ)).

    The RVM fits a Bernoulli likelihood with a logistic link and independent Gaussian priors wᵢ ~ N(0, 1/αᵢ). Each round finds the posterior mode w, approximates the posterior covariance Σ from the local curvature, and updates αᵢ ← (1 − αᵢΣᵢᵢ)/wᵢ². This demo uses 40 approximate evidence updates and removes a center when its updated precision reaches 10,000. Its boundary uses the fitted posterior-mode score.
